# The/Nudge Institute – Financial Performance Dashboard

A comprehensive financial reporting dashboard showcasing the income, expenditure, efficiency, and financial sustainability of **The/Nudge Institute** across four financial years (FY 2021–22 to FY 2024–25).

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a **Financial Performance Dashboard** designed to provide a transparent, data-driven view of The/Nudge Institute’s financial health. It consolidates key financial metrics, trends, and ratios into a single visual report suitable for leadership, donors, auditors, and internal finance teams.

All monetary values are represented in **Indian Rupees (₹) Crores**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Track income and expenditure trends over multiple years  
- Measure program efficiency using standard nonprofit metrics  
- Analyze expenditure composition  
- Monitor balance sheet growth and long-term sustainability  
- Support informed decision-making and financial governance  

---

## 📊 Key Financial Highlights (FY 2021–25)

| Metric | Value |
|------|------|
| **Total Income** | ₹261.23 Cr |
| **Total Expenditure** | ₹256.30 Cr |
| **Program Spending Ratio** | 86.18% |
| **Corpus Funds** | ₹32.80 Cr |

---

## 📈 Dashboard Components

### 1️⃣ Total Income
Represents cumulative income over four financial years, including grants, donations, and program funding.

**Insight:**  
Consistent income growth reflects strong donor confidence and funding continuity.

---

### 2️⃣ Total Expenditure
Captures overall spending incurred during the same period.

**Insight:**  
Expenditure growth closely aligns with income, indicating disciplined financial planning and controlled costs.

---

### 3️⃣ Program Spending Ratio (86.18%)
A critical nonprofit efficiency metric showing the proportion of expenditure directly allocated to program initiatives.

**Why it matters:**
- Demonstrates mission focus  
- Builds donor trust  
- Exceeds common nonprofit benchmarks (75–85%)

---

### 4️⃣ Income vs Expenditure Trend (4 Years)
Visual comparison of annual income and expenditure from FY 2021–22 to FY 2024–25.

**Key Observations:**
- Upward trend in both income and spending  
- Income consistently exceeds expenditure  
- Indicates healthy operational surplus  

---

### 5️⃣ Expenditure Breakdown by Type
Expenses are categorized into:
- Program Initiatives  
- Employee Benefits  
- Administrative Expenses  

**Insight:**  
Program initiatives account for the largest share (≈85–88%), highlighting impact-first spending.

---

### 6️⃣ Balance Sheet Growth
Tracks changes in:
- Cash & Cash Equivalents  
- Corpus Funds  
- Reserves & Surplus  

**Insight:**  
Steady growth across all components reflects improving liquidity, reserves, and long-term financial resilience.

---

## 🧮 Key Financial Metrics Explained

- **Program Spending Ratio** = Program Expenses ÷ Total Expenditure  
- **Corpus Funds** = Long-term capital reserved for sustainability  
- **Reserves & Surplus** = Accumulated operational surpluses
